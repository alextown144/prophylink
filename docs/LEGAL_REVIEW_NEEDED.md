# Legal Review Needed

This document flags issues that require qualified legal or compliance review before public launch. It is not legal advice.

- Worker classification and whether platform participants are employees, contractors, or independent marketplace users.
- Washington-specific dental staffing and professional licensing rules.
- Whether ProphyLink needs staffing agency registration or other business licensing.
- Credential representations and limits of manual verification.
- Liability allocation among offices, professionals, and ProphyLink.
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- Cancellation, no-show, and dispute policies.
- Payment wording and subscription cancellation language.
- Whether any messaging or notes could create PHI risk.
- Continuing education claims and accreditation requirements if CE is added.
