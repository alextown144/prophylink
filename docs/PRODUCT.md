# Product Brief

## Product

ProphyLink connects dental offices with dental professionals for temporary staffing coverage.

Launch professions:

- Dental Hygienist
- Dental Assistant

Launch market:

- Tri-Cities, Washington

The product must stay flexible enough to expand to additional dental roles and additional markets without major schema changes.

## Core Workflows

### Office Staffing

An office can search available professionals, post an open shift, invite professionals, receive interest, approve a match, and confirm coverage.

### Professional Shift Discovery

A dental professional can set availability, browse open shifts, express interest, and receive office invitations.

### Coverage Exchange

An employed professional can request coverage for a shift they need off. For MVP, this is primarily a professional-to-professional connection: the original professional is responsible for arranging permission and approval with their own office outside ProphyLink. ProphyLink should help the original professional find an available replacement professional, not represent that the office has approved the replacement.

## MVP Priorities

- Trustworthy professional and office profiles
- Extremely simple availability entry
- Clear shift search and posting
- Manual credential review
- Admin concierge control
- Useful empty states for a small early marketplace
- Mobile-friendly workflows

## MVP Non-Goals

- Payroll
- Tax withholding
- Worker benefits
- Patient records or PHI
- AI matching
- Native mobile apps
- Full continuing education LMS
- Practice-management software integrations
- Legal conclusions about employment classification

## Success Criteria

The MVP is successful when a hygienist, assistant, office, coverage exchange, and admin credential-review workflow can each be demonstrated end to end with fictional demo data.
